Why We Are Asking

The EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) requires companies to disclose value chain impacts under the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS), starting with reporting years from 2024 and extending to more organisations through 2026 and beyond. Scope 3 accounts for 71% of our total carbon footprint, location-based, 2023 base year — the majority of it embedded in the products and services our Tier 1 suppliers provide. We cannot reach Net-Zero by 2045 without visibility into that footprint.

This assessment is how we build that visibility. It asks each Tier 1 supplier to report on climate and decarbonisation, pollution and PFAS, water and marine resources, circular economy and waste, biodiversity, and social and governance performance. The responsibility is shared: our target depends on your data, and your response shapes how we work together going forward.
